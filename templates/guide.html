How does it work?

Step 1: Set Your Timer!

  • Hold up 1–5 fingers to choose countdown length

Step 2: Start the Countdown!

  • Say "START" to begin the countdown (Closed fist gesture to reset)

Step 3: Strike Your Poses!

  • Get ready for 4 photos — the countdown repeats automatically

Step 4: Review Your Photos!

  • Say "YES" to retake or "NO" to continue

Step 5: Choose Your Design!

  • Say "CLASSIC", "FUN", or "ELEGANT" to pick your photo strip design

Step 6: Apply a Filter!

  • Say "NORMAL" for colour or "BLACK AND WHITE"

Step 7: Download Your Strip!

  • Click download to save your photo strip to your device

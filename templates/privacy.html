Privacy & Data Notice

Camera Access

VisionBooth requires access to your device's camera to capture photos. Your camera feed is processed locally and is never stored or transmitted to external servers unless you explicitly download your photos.

Microphone Access

VisionBooth uses your microphone to recognize voice commands. Audio is processed locally and not recorded or stored after command recognition.

Photo Storage

Your photo strips are stored locally in the sessions folder. You have full control and can delete them at any time.

Gesture Recognition

Hand gestures are detected using local computer vision. Gesture data is processed in real-time on your device and not stored or transmitted.

Contactless & Hygienic

VisionBooth is a completely contactless experience. Control everything using only gestures and voice — no buttons to touch.
By proceeding, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this privacy notice and agree to the use of camera and microphone.

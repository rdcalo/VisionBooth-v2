Privacy & Data Notice

Camera Access

VisionBooth requires access to your device's camera to capture photos. Your camera feed is processed locally on your device and is never stored or transmitted to external servers unless you explicitly download your photos.

Microphone Access

VisionBooth uses your microphone to recognize voice commands ("START", "YES", "NO", etc.). Audio is processed locally and not recorded or stored after command recognition is complete.

Photo Storage

Your photo strips are stored locally on this device in the sessions folder. You have full control over your photos and can delete them at any time. We do not upload, analyze, or share your images without your explicit consent.

Gesture Recognition

Hand gestures are detected using local computer vision technology. Gesture data is processed in real-time on your device and not stored or transmitted.

Contactless & Hygienic

VisionBooth is designed as a completely contactless experience. You control the entire photo booth using only gestures and voice commands—no buttons to touch, no screens to interact with manually.
By proceeding, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this privacy notice and agree to the use of camera and microphone.
